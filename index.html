What it does

Save anything

One click or Ctrl+Shift+S. mem summarizes the page, tags it, and files it.

Ask in plain English

“What did I read about solar cells?” Answers come from your own history, with sources.

Never miss a deadline

On chat sites you opt into, mem spots “due Friday at 3pm” and offers a reminder. An on-device AI checks each one so timestamps don’t bother you.

One timeline

Optional read-only Google connections (Drive, Gmail, YouTube, Calendar, Classroom) fold into the same memory.

Private by design.

Your memory belongs on your machine, not on someone else’s server.

  • Everything stays local. Stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. No mem server, no account.
  • On-device AI by default. Chrome’s built-in model does the work — or bring your own Gemini or OpenAI key.
  • Conversation watching is off by default. You opt in per site, and you can turn it off just as easily.
  • Your data, your call. Export or erase everything at any time.
